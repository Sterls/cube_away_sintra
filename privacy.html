Privacy Policy

Whoosh Blocks · Last updated 30 October 2026

Whoosh Blocks is a puzzle game made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game has no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and your progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, trophies and settings (sound and vibration) are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. Clearing the game's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.

The optional leaderboard

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to join the leaderboard. If you join, the game sends:

  • the name you choose. Please don't use your real name;
  • the furthest level you have beaten;
  • a random ID created on your device, so your score can be updated. It isn't linked to you, your device or any account.

Names and levels are shown publicly on the leaderboard. The random ID is never shown. This information is stored by our database provider, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. We use it only to run the leaderboard, and our legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by asking us to delete your entry. Inappropriate names may be removed.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, the servers that deliver the game (GitHub Pages) and the leaderboard (Supabase) may briefly record technical information such as your IP address and browser type in their logs, to keep their services running and secure. We don't use this information and don't receive it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The leaderboard is optional and asks players not to use real names.

How long we keep information

Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard runs, and may be deleted after 12 months without activity. You can ask for your entry to be deleted at any time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, or object to how it's used. To make a request, email the developer at the contact address shown on the game's Google Play or App Store listing. If you joined the leaderboard, include the name you used. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
